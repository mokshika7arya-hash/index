The Real Truth

Why running feels awful when you start on your own

If you've tried running in the past and quit after three days, it wasn't your body's fault.

1

You ran way too fast

Most people step out their front door and sprint at their high school PE speed. Within 90 seconds your chest is on fire, your throat burns, and you tell yourself: "I'm just not built for this."

2

Your ego wouldn't let you walk

We've been taught that walking is "cheating." In reality, walking intervals are how Olympic coaches train athletes: they let your heart recover so you build endurance without burning out.

3

You ran on consecutive days

Muscles adapt in days; knee tendons and shin bones need 48 hours to rebuild. Trying to run every day without rest causes stiffness and joint pain, knocking you out for weeks.
